Reading Tips

How to Score Higher in IELTS Academic Reading CBT

Expert strategies for the computer-based IELTS Academic Reading test.

Skim First, Then Scan

In the IELTS CBT, skim each passage for 2–3 minutes to understand structure and main ideas. Then scan for specific answers. Never read every word — it wastes precious time across 3 long academic passages.

Use the Highlight Tool Strategically

The computer based IELTS reading highlight tool is your best friend. Mark topic sentences, key names, dates, and paragraph themes as you skim. This saves scrolling back to re-read during questions.

True/False/Not Given — Exact Meaning

The most common trap in IELTS Academic Reading: NOT GIVEN means the text neither confirms nor contradicts the statement. Do not use outside knowledge — base every answer only on what the passage says.

Matching Headings — Read Topic Sentences

For Matching Headings questions, read only the first and last sentence of each paragraph. The answer is almost always in the topic sentence. Try to match the meaning, not the exact words.

20 Minutes Per Passage

Budget exactly 20 minutes per passage. Passage 3 is always the hardest. If you spend 25+ minutes on Passage 1 or 2, you risk running out of time. Set a mental timer and move on — always attempt every question.

Copy Answers Exactly From the Text

For completion questions in IELTS CBT Reading, copy the exact words from the passage — do not paraphrase. Check spelling carefully. Word limits (e.g. "no more than TWO words") are strictly enforced.

Is IELTS Academic Reading computer based test different from paper based?
The question types and content are identical. The main difference is the interface — in the Computer Based Test (CBT), the passage appears on the left panel and questions on the right, both scrolling independently. You also have access to a highlight tool, a question navigator, and a flag/review feature not available on paper.
How many passages are in IELTS Academic Reading CBT?
There are 3 passages in the IELTS Academic Reading test, with a total of 40 questions to be answered in 60 minutes. Passage 1 is the easiest, Passage 3 is the most difficult. Each passage is between 700–1,000 words drawn from academic journals, books, magazines, or newspapers.
Can I highlight text in the IELTS computer based reading test?
Yes. The official IELTS CBT interface includes a highlight tool that lets you mark key phrases in the passage. You can highlight in multiple colours. Our practice tests replicate this exact feature so you can train with it before test day.
