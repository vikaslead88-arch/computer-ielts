Academic Reading Tips

How to Score Higher in IELTS Academic Reading CBT

Expert strategies for the computer-based IELTS Academic Reading test.

Skim First, Read Second

In the CBT format, skim each passage for the main idea before reading questions. The left panel stays fixed — scan headings and topic sentences first to build a mental map of the text.

20 Minutes Per Passage

The 60-minute timer auto-submits in the real IELTS CBT. Aim for 20 minutes per passage. Use the question navigator to skip difficult questions and return later — never get stuck on one question.

Use the CBT Highlight Tool

The IELTS on computer interface includes a highlight function. Mark key phrases as you read — this saves scrolling time and is crucial for matching headings and TFNG questions.

Paraphrase is the Real Test

Academic Reading questions rarely use the exact words from the passage. Train yourself to recognise synonyms and paraphrased ideas — this is the core skill tested in IELTS Academic CBT.

YES/NO/NOT GIVEN vs T/F/NG

Yes/No/Not Given tests the writer's opinion. True/False/Not Given tests factual information. Knowing which type you are answering is critical for accuracy in the CBT academic reading test.

Passage 3 is Always Hardest

In IELTS Academic Reading, Passage 3 is always the most complex — longer and more abstract. Budget extra time here. If running low on time, make educated guesses rather than leaving blanks (no negative marking).

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything students ask about IELTS Academic Reading Computer Based Test

Is the IELTS Academic Reading CBT different from the paper-based test?
The question types and content are identical. The key difference is the interface — in the Computer Based Test (CBT), the passage appears on the left panel and questions on the right, both scrolling independently. You can also highlight text directly on screen in the CBT version. Importantly, there is no separate answer transfer time in the CBT — answers are typed in directly as you go.
How many passages are in IELTS Academic Reading?
There are 3 passages in the IELTS Academic Reading test, with a total of 40 questions to be answered in 60 minutes. The passages increase in difficulty — Passage 1 is the easiest, Passage 3 is always the most challenging. Aim to spend approximately 20 minutes per passage.
Can I highlight text in the IELTS computer based reading test?
Yes. The official IELTS CBT interface includes a highlight tool that lets you mark key phrases directly in the passage. Our practice tests on computerielts.com include this exact feature so you can build the habit before your real exam.
What happens when the 60-minute timer runs out in IELTS CBT?
In the real IELTS Computer Based Test, the test auto-submits when the timer reaches zero. There is no extra time. A warning appears with 5 minutes remaining. Our practice tests replicate this exactly. There is no negative marking in IELTS, so a guess is always better than a blank.
What question types appear in IELTS Academic Reading?
IELTS Academic Reading includes up to 15 question types across the 3 passages:

Opinion-based: Yes/No/Not Given
Factual: True/False/Not Given
Matching: Matching Information, Matching Headings, Matching Features, Matching Sentence Endings
Completion: Sentence, Summary, Note, Table, Flow-chart, Diagram Label Completion
Other: Multiple Choice, Short Answer Questions
How is the band score calculated for IELTS Academic Reading?
