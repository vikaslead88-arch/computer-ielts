🎧 About This Listening Test

This Computer-Based IELTS Listening Practice Test 4 replicates the official IELTS CBT listening examination. Part 1 is a conversation about property rental advice covering accommodation and viewing arrangements. Part 2 is a monologue about health centres and doctors. Part 3 is an academic discussion about a teacher named Jonathan Briggs and his teaching experience. Part 4 is an academic lecture on back pain research and treatment.

All official question types are included: notes completion, form completion, short answer, choose-two, and multiple choice. The 40-minute countdown timer mirrors the real test duration.
