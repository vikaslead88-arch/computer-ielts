Writing Task 2 Tips

How to Score Higher in IELTS Writing Task 2

Quick examiner-approved strategies for each essay type.

Always Address All Parts

Task Response is worth 25% of your score. Read the question twice and identify every sub-task — missing any part immediately lowers your band.

Structure: 4 Paragraphs

Introduction → Body 1 → Body 2 → Conclusion. Each body paragraph needs one clear main idea, development, and a specific example or reason.

Spend 5 Minutes Planning

5 minutes planning + 35 minutes writing beats 40 minutes of unplanned writing every time. A planned essay has clearer argument flow and higher CC scores.

Vary Your Vocabulary

For Band 7+ Lexical Resource, use synonyms naturally — not forced. Repeating "important" 6 times hurts your score. Use "significant", "crucial", "essential" contextually.

Write at Least 250 Words

Under 250 words is penalised under Task Response. Aim for 270–300 words. Padding with irrelevant sentences also penalises — quality matters more than quantity above 250.

Leave 3 Minutes to Check

In the last 3 minutes, scan for subject-verb agreement, article errors (a/the), and spelling. These are the most common CBT marking deductions at Band 6–7 level.
